Trading Strategies Playbook

Proven frameworks spanning technical breakouts, mean reversion, fundamental catalysts, and quantitative factor models.

Trend Momentum Breakout
Win Rate ~54%

Timeframe: 4H / Daily • R:R Target: 1 : 2.5+

Identifies multi-week consolidation channels. Enters on volume-backed break above 50-day high with ATR expansion.

Setup Rules:
  • Price trading above 200 EMA & 50 EMA
  • Volume on breakout > 150% 20-day average
  • Stop loss set 1.5 × ATR below entry candle
Technical Verified Edge
Liquidity Sweep & FVG Fill
Win Rate ~62%

Timeframe: 15m / 1H • R:R Target: 1 : 3.0+

Capitalizes on stops triggered outside Asian or London sessions. Enters on market structure shift with Fair Value Gap confluence.

Setup Rules:
  • Clean sweep of previous session high/low
  • Aggressive Displacement creating an imbalance
  • Limit order entry at 50% FVG retracement
Institutional High R:R
Pairs Stat-Arb Mean Reversion
Win Rate ~68%

Timeframe: 1H / Daily • R:R Target: 1 : 1.5

Cointegrated asset pair (e.g. KO vs. PEP). Calculates rolling Z-score of spread; enters when Z-score exceeds ±2.0.

Setup Rules:
  • Engle-Granger cointegration p-value < 0.05
  • Spread Z-Score > +2.0 (Short Spread)
  • Take profit at Z-Score = 0.0 mean cross

Core Performance Math
Formulas

Sharpe Ratio: \( \frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p} \) • Measure of excess return per unit of total risk.

Sortino Ratio: \( \frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_d} \) • Penalizes only downside standard deviation.

Z-Score (Spread): \( \frac{x_t - \mu}{\sigma} \) • Normalizes price spread for mean reversion.

Kelly Criterion: \( f^* = p - \frac{q}{b} \) • Optimal capital allocation fraction.

Options & Derivatives

Options Greeks, implied volatility surfaces, non-linear payoff curves, and risk-defined hedging structures.

Δ Delta

Sensitivity of option price to a \$1 change in underlying asset. Also proxies probability of expiring In-The-Money (ITM).

Γ Gamma

Rate of change of Delta for a \$1 move in the underlying. Highest for At-The-Money (ATM) options approaching expiration.

Θ Theta

Time decay: dollar amount an option loses each calendar day, assuming all other factors remain constant. Accelerates in last 30 days.

ν Vega

Sensitivity of option price to a 1% change in Implied Volatility (IV). Long options are positive Vega; short options are negative Vega.

Non-Negotiable Risk Rules

The 1% - 2% Rule

Never risk more than 1% to 2% of total account equity on any single execution. Surviving a 10-trade losing streak leaves 82%–90% of capital intact.

Daily Circuit Breaker

If account drawdown reaches 3% in a single trading session, stop trading immediately for 24 hours to prevent revenge trading and tilt.

Positive Asymmetric R:R

Only enter setups where potential reward is at least 2.0× greater than the risk. At a 1:2 R:R, you only need a 34% win rate to break even.

Glossary & Knowledge Base

Searchable institutional dictionary, trading vocabulary, reference notes, and personal research archive.

Arbitrage
Execution

Simultaneous purchase and sale of equivalent assets in different markets to exploit pricing discrepancies without market directional risk.

Beta (β)
Risk Metric

Measure of an asset's volatility and systematic risk in comparison to the broader benchmark market (such as the S&P 500, which has Beta = 1.0).

Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD)
Order Flow

Running total of market buy volume minus market sell volume executed at the bid and ask, revealing hidden institutional absorption.

Maximum Drawdown (MDD)
Risk

The peak-to-trough decline during a specific period for an investment portfolio or strategy, measured as a percentage of the peak value.

Statistical Edge
Probability

Positive mathematical expectancy over a large series of trades: Expectancy = (Win% × Avg Win) − (Loss% × Avg Loss).

Fair Value Gap (FVG)
Price Action

A three-candle price imbalance where candle 1 high and candle 3 low do not overlap, leaving an unfilled liquidity pocket in candle 2.

Implied Volatility (IV)
Options

Market expectation of future price volatility derived backward from the Black-Scholes option pricing model. High IV inflates option premiums.

Liquidity Sweep / Raid
Order Flow

Intentional push beyond major swing highs or lows to fill large institutional orders against retail stop-losses before reversing direction.

Slippage
Execution

The difference between the expected price of a trade and the actual filled price, common during fast news releases or illiquid hours.
