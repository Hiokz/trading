Technical Analysis

Visual price action, momentum indicators, chart geometry, and volume-weighted market profiles.

Trend Following & Direction
4 Indicators
Simple Moving Average (SMA)
Trend Baseline

Calculates the arithmetic mean price over a specified lookback window. Filters out market noise to reveal underlying directional trends and serves as institutional dynamic support and resistance.

Key Setups: 20 SMA (Short-term), 50 SMA (Medium-term), 200 SMA (Macro baseline / Golden & Death Cross).
Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
Fast Trend

Applies an exponential weighting multiplier to recent prices, reducing lag relative to the SMA. Enables faster identification of trend transitions, dynamic pullback retests, and moving average cross systems.

Key Setups: 9 & 21 EMA (Momentum cross triggers), 50 & 200 EMA (Structural trend bias), Moving Average Ribbons.
Supertrend
Trend & Trailing Stop

A hybrid trend-following overlay built from the median price $(H+L)/2$ combined with an ATR volatility band. Color transitions delineate bullish (green) vs. bearish (red) regimes and provide an objective trailing stop.

Key Setups: ATR Length: 10, Multiplier: 3.0 (Equities/Forex); 7 / 3.0 (High-volatility / Crypto).
Average Directional Index (ADX)
Trend Strength

Quantifies the strength of an ongoing trend on a 0–100 scale regardless of price direction. Derived from smoothed $+DI$ and $-DI$ vectors. Distinguishes high-probability trending moves from choppy sideways consolidations.

Key Levels: > 25 (Strong trend confirmed), > 40 (Climax trend / exhaustion risk), < 20 (Ranging / chop regime).
Momentum & Oscillators
3 Indicators
Relative Strength Index (RSI)
Momentum

Measures the velocity and magnitude of directional price movements on a 0–100 scale. Flags overbought and oversold extremes and highlights trend fatigue or continuation through regular and hidden divergences.

Key Levels: Length: 14 | >70 Overbought, <30 Oversold, 50 Midline regime filter.
Stochastic RSI
Fast Oscillator

Applies the Stochastic oscillator formula to RSI values rather than raw price bars. Produces a sensitive indicator designed to detect micro-cycle turning points, momentum shifts, and short-term swing reversals.

Key Levels: %K: 3, %D: 3, RSI Length: 14, Stoch Length: 14 | >80 Overbought, <20 Oversold.
MACD
Trend & Momentum

Plots the spread between the 12 EMA and 26 EMA alongside a 9-period signal line and histogram. Histogram expansion signals accelerating momentum; signal line crossovers and zero-line crosses confirm structural trend shifts.

Key Setups: Fast: 12, Slow: 26, Signal: 9 | Signal line cross, Zero-line cross, Histogram divergence.
Volatility & Price Channels
3 Indicators
Average True Range (ATR)
Volatility

Measures market volatility in absolute currency points by calculating the true range across bars, accounting for overnight gaps. Provides an objective basis for dynamic stop-loss sizing and risk allocation.

Key Setups: Length: 14 | Dynamic stop placement (e.g. 1.5x–2.5x ATR), Volatility breakout filters.
Bollinger Bands
Statistical Bands

Envelopes price with a 20 SMA middle line and upper/lower bands placed 2 standard deviations away. Identifies volatility contractions ("The Squeeze") preceding powerful expansions and flags mean-reversion exhaustion zones.

Key Setups: Basis: 20 SMA, Multiplier: 2.0σ | Band Squeeze breakouts, Band Walking, Mean reversion.
Donchian Channels
Range & Breakout

Plots the highest high and lowest low over an $N$-period lookback window, with a median midpoint line. Forms the cornerstone of systematic trend breakout strategies, historically proven by the legendary Turtle Traders.

Key Setups: 20 Periods (Classic Turtle entry), 10 Periods (Exit trailing stop), 55 Periods (Long-term trend).
Volume & Liquidity
2 Indicators
Volume
Market Fuel

Displays the total shares or contracts traded during each interval. Serves as the ultimate truth detector for price action: genuine institutional moves expand volume, while low-volume breakouts warn of weak conviction and bull/bear traps.

Key Setups: 20-period Volume SMA overlay | Volume Breakout expansion, Climax exhaustion, Dry-up pullbacks.
VWAP
Institutional Anchor

Volume-Weighted Average Price calculates the cumulative dollar value divided by volume traded from session open. Benchmark for institutional execution algorithms and market makers. Standard deviation bands act as dynamic support/resistance.

Key Setups: Session Anchored (Daily / Weekly / Monthly) | Intraday bias (Above = Bullish, Below = Bearish), ±1σ & ±2σ Bands.

Fundamental Analysis

Macroeconomic indicators, central bank monetary policy, corporate financial statements, and intrinsic valuation models.

Macro & Corporate
Macroeconomic Analysis
10 Macro Indicators
Consumer Price Index (CPI)
Inflation

Measures the average change over time in prices paid by consumers for a basket of goods and services. Headline CPI tracks broad cost pressures, while Core CPI excludes volatile food and energy to reflect sticky underlying inflation.

Key Metrics: Headline vs. Core CPI (MoM & YoY), 2.0% Fed Target, Shelter & Supercore services.
Interest Rates & Policy Rates
Monetary Policy

The benchmark risk-free overnight lending rate established by central banks (e.g. Federal Funds Rate, ECB Deposit Rate). Serves as the fundamental discount rate for all financial assets; higher rates tighten financial conditions and compress equity valuation multiples.

Key Drivers: Effective Fed Funds Rate (EFFR), Discount Window, Terminal Rate pricing.
Gross Domestic Product (GDP)
Economic Output

The total monetary value of all finished goods and services produced within a nation over a quarterly period. Real GDP adjusts for inflation to measure genuine economic growth; two consecutive quarters of contraction signal a technical recession.

Key Releases: Advance, Second, Final QoQ annualized prints, Consumer Spending (PCE).
Employment & Labor Capacity
Labor Market

Assesses the aggregate health of the domestic labor force through the headline U-3 unemployment rate, labor force participation, and JOLTS job openings. Strong labor markets underpin consumer resilience but can trigger wage-push inflationary spirals.

Key Gauges: U-3 Unemployment Rate, Participation Rate, JOLTS Job Openings-to-Unemployed ratio.
Inflation & Core PCE
Fed Preferred Gauge

The Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index tracks price changes across consumer purchases with dynamic basket weighting. Core PCE is the Federal Reserve's primary metric for assessing compliance with its 2.0% inflation mandate.

Key Target: 2.0% Annualized Target | Supercore PCE (services ex-housing & energy).
Monetary Policy & Balance Sheet (QE/QT)
Liquidity Flows

The direct expansion or contraction of central bank assets. Quantitative Easing (QE) purchases bonds to inject cash reserves and depress long-term yields. Quantitative Tightening (QT) allows bond maturities to roll off, absorbing excess market liquidity.

Liquidity Levers: Fed Total Assets (WALCL), Reverse Repo (RRP), Treasury General Account (TGA).
Producer Price Index (PPI)
Wholesale Inflation

Evaluates price changes from the perspective of domestic producers and manufacturers. Because raw material and intermediate processing cost increases are passed down to end consumers, PPI acts as a premier leading indicator for consumer inflation.

Leading Signals: Final Demand PPI, Core PPI (ex-food & energy), Pipeline Goods Inflation.
FOMC & Fed Rate Decisions
Central Bank Policy

The Federal Open Market Committee holds 8 scheduled annual meetings to determine interest rate targets. Traders closely dissect the policy statement, Summary of Economic Projections (Dot Plot), and the Fed Chair's post-meeting press conference.

Key Catalysts: Fed Rate Decision, Dot Plot median expectations, FOMC Meeting Minutes.
Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)
Jobs Catalyst

Released on the first Friday of every month by the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS). Measures net monthly job creation across non-agricultural businesses. Generates significant instant volatility across the US Dollar, bond yields, and stock indices.

Market Movers: Net Payroll Additions (+/- 150k baseline), Prior Month Revisions, Average Hourly Earnings.
Treasury Yields & Curve Inversions
Recession Model

Sovereign bond yields across 2Y, 10Y, and 30Y tenors. The 10Y - 2Y yield curve spread is Wall Street's premier recession indicator: yield inversions (short rates exceeding long rates) have accurately preceded every US recession since 1955.

Curve Spreads: 10Y - 2Y Spread (Inversion vs. Steepening), 10Y - 3M Spread, 10Y Benchmark discount rate.
Company Fundamental Analysis
8 Corporate Pillars
Revenue & Top-Line Growth
Top-Line

Gross dollar receipts generated from business operations before deducting operating costs. Evaluates commercial adoption, sector market share gains, and sales momentum across quarterly (QoQ) and annual (YoY) reporting cycles.

Key Metrics: Revenue Growth YoY %, Organic vs. Acquisition Growth, Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).
Earnings & Profit Margins
Bottom-Line

Net income remaining after all cost of goods sold (COGS), operating expenses, interest, depreciation, and corporate taxes. Margin trends demonstrate corporate pricing power, cost discipline, and defensive economic moat durability.

Key Margins: Gross Margin (Pricing power), Operating Margin (EBIT), Net Profit Margin (Final conversion).
Earnings Per Share (EPS)
Per-Share Metric

Net profit allocated to each individual share of outstanding common stock. Wall Street's primary benchmark for quarterly earnings beats and misses; unexpected beats and forward guidance upgrades drive immediate equity re-ratings.

Key Metrics: GAAP EPS, Normalized Non-GAAP EPS, Diluted EPS (accounting for options & convertibles).
Price-to-Earnings (P/E) & Multiples
Valuation Multiple

Compares current share price against annual per-share earnings, measuring how much capital the market pays per dollar of profit. Trailing P/E assesses historical results, Forward P/E prices future growth, and PEG incorporates projected growth rates.

Key Ratios: Trailing P/E (TTM), Forward P/E (NTM), PEG Ratio (P/E / Annual EPS Growth; < 1.0 undervalued).
Cash Flow & Free Cash Flow (FCF)
Liquidity Truth

While accounting net income is subject to non-cash accruals, Free Cash Flow represents actual cash generated from operations minus Capital Expenditures (CapEx). FCF funds dividends, share buybacks, acquisitions, and debt paydown.

Formulas: FCF = Operating Cash Flow - CapEx | FCF Conversion Rate (FCF / Net Income), FCF Yield %.
Debt & Financial Solvency
Balance Sheet Risk

Evaluates corporate debt obligations, liquidity reserves, and balance sheet solvency. While modest leverage amplifies returns during economic expansions, excessive debt loads trigger severe insolvency risk when interest rates climb.

Solvency Checks: Debt-to-Equity (D/E), Net Debt / EBITDA (< 2.5x safe), Interest Coverage Ratio (EBIT / Interest).
Intrinsic Valuation & DCF Models
Fair Value

Calculates a business's true intrinsic worth independent of market volatility. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) models project 5–10 years of future cash flows discounted to present value using WACC, establishing an objective Margin of Safety.

Methodologies: Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), Enterprise Value (EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales), Margin of Safety (> 25%).
Capital Efficiency (ROIC & ROE)
Moat Strength

Measures management's ability to efficiently reinvest capital into high-return projects. Companies with Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) consistently exceeding their Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) compound wealth and possess wide moats.

Key Benchmarks: ROIC (Return on Invested Capital), ROE (Return on Equity), Economic Value Added (ROIC - WACC).
